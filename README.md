# Autheo Tokenization-as-a-Service — strategy & plan

> **You choose the asset. You choose the destination. Autheo handles the chains.**

Autheo as the infrastructure connecting traditional finance, decentralized markets and tokenized assets — giving banks, financial institutions and institutional investors tokenization, compliant trading and settlement **without Autheo becoming a bank**. Autheo supplies technology, compliance enforcement, interoperability (L0 Mesh) and settlement rails; regulated activity runs through licensed partners.

## Pillars
- **Real-world asset tokenization** — bonds, equities, private credit, real estate, funds.
- **Institutional DEX capabilities** — compliant secondary trading, liquidity access, programmable settlement (DvP).
- **Traditional banking integration** — core banking, custody and payment networks through enterprise APIs.
- **Embedded regulatory compliance** — KYC/AML, sanctions, jurisdictional rules, transaction policies, auditable execution.
- **Cross-chain interoperability** — issue, manage and settle across supported chains through L0 Mesh.
- **THEO utility** — network and settlement fees, staking, where appropriate.
