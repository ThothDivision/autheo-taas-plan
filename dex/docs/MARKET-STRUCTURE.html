Market structure

Open tierInstitutional tier

AssetsTHEO, majors, stablecoins, permissionless tokensTaaS-issued RWAs, tokenized deposits, permissioned stablecoins

Order types

Limit, market (with protection band), RFQ (request → firm quotes → accept), iceberg, good-till-time, batch-auction limit, and intents ("deliver ≥ N units of X on chain Z by T"). Agents may submit any of these within the limits of their DID delegation (scope, budget, venue, asset class).

Cross-chain routeper L0 Mesh hop

Staking THEO lowers fees (tiers in THEO). Institutions that cannot hold tokens pay in fiat/stable, auto-converted.

Liquidity programme

  • Registered market makers on the institutional tier: quoting obligations (spread, size, uptime) for rebates.
  • Issuer-sponsored liquidity at launch of an RWA (TaaS issuance can reserve inventory for a designated market maker).
  • Open-tier LP incentives in THEO, time-limited and tied to depth, not raw TVL.
