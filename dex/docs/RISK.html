Risk & compliance

RiskMitigation
Operating an unlicensed exchangeInstitutional tier operated by / with a licensed ATS/MTF/RMO partner per jurisdiction; Autheo provides technology. Open tier limited to non-security tokens.
Securities leaking into open poolsTransfer hooks reject non-eligible addresses including pool contracts; listing policy per asset.
Smart-contract failureAudits, formal verification of settlement and hook contracts, staged limits, pause by issuer/venue operator.
Thin RWA liquidityBatch auctions, RFQ, designated market makers, NAV-anchored price bands.
Oracle manipulationMultiple sources, staleness and deviation breakers, zkTLS-attested sources for off-chain facts.
MEV / front-runningFrequent batch auctions, sealed order submission, solver competition with bonds.
Cross-chain riskCanonical supply on L1, attested mint/burn via L0 Mesh, per-chain caps, rules enforced on destinations.
Agent misuseDID delegation chains with scope/budget/venue limits, receipts, revocation.
QuantumHybrid ML-DSA signatures on settlement and audit, hybrid ML-KEM transport; tunable profiles per institution.
PrivacyCredentials prove eligibility without PII on-chain; confidential balances studied for institutions.
