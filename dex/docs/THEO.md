# THEO economics

| Use | Mechanism | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading fees | Denominated in THEO; fiat/stable payers auto-converted | Single fee asset across tiers and chains |
| Fee tiers | Stake THEO → 0 / 15 / 35 % discount | Aligns heavy users with the network |
| Solver & relayer bonds | Solvers and L0 Mesh relayers stake THEO, slashed for failed or dishonest routes | Security budget for routing |
| Market-maker rebates | Paid in THEO for meeting quoting obligations | Depth on the institutional tier |
| LP incentives | Time-boxed THEO emissions tied to depth near mid | Bootstraps open-tier liquidity |
| Governance | Listing standards for the open tier, fee parameters | Institutional-tier listings follow the partner venue's rules, not token votes |

Where THEO is **not** used: as the cash leg of regulated trades, as collateral for RWAs, or as a
condition for institutions to access the venue. Fee values are configuration for modelling, not an offer.
