THEO economics

UseMechanismWhy
Trading feesDenominated in THEO; fiat/stable payers auto-convertedSingle fee asset across tiers and chains
Fee tiersStake THEO → 0 / 15 / 35 % discountAligns heavy users with the network
Solver & relayer bondsSolvers and L0 Mesh relayers stake THEO, slashed for failed or dishonest routesSecurity budget for routing
Market-maker rebatesPaid in THEO for meeting quoting obligationsDepth on the institutional tier
LP incentivesTime-boxed THEO emissions tied to depth near midBootstraps open-tier liquidity
GovernanceListing standards for the open tier, fee parametersInstitutional-tier listings follow the partner venue's rules, not token votes

Where THEO is not used: as the cash leg of regulated trades, as collateral for RWAs, or as a condition for institutions to access the venue. Fee values are configuration for modelling, not an offer.
