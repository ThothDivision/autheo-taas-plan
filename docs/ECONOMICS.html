Economics (indicative)

Every number on this page is computed from the inputs in the first table. The inputs are assumptions for discussion, not benchmarked prices or a forecast; change an input and recompute. Costs are not estimated here (see decision D-09 in Decisions and risks).

What moves the result most: issuance count and size dominate year 1; turnover dominates every later year, which is why the plan puts secondary liquidity (designated market makers, anchor assets) ahead of new features — see DEX integration.

Where THEO flows

  • Fees are quoted in fiat and settled in THEO; institutions that cannot hold tokens pay fiat and a partner converts.
  • Staking tiers give fee discounts; L0 Mesh relayers and attesters stake THEO as their security bond.
  • THEO is not the cash leg of regulated instruments (tokenized deposits / regulated stablecoins are).

Not modelled

Partner revenue share (bank float, venue fees), white-label licences, THEO price, staking discounts, operating and compliance costs, and the time from pilot to the first live issuance. These are open until D-09 is decided.
