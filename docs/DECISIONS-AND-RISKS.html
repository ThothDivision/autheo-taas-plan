Premortem (R0)

"It is two years from now and TaaS failed. Why?" — the answers that became decisions or risks above: there was no bank for the cash leg (R-01, D-03); a regulator saw Autheo as the venue (R-02, D-04); listings had no buyers (R-05); a contract bug froze an asset (R-03); the off-chain and on-chain rules disagreed in production (R-04); the plan's fees were never tested against the market (D-09); the build ran a year late because the first estimate was the inside view (R-10).

Who the plan leaves out

The plan is written for issuers, banks and venues. Two parties carry risk without a seat: end investors of the institutions (their positions are frozen by pauses and sanctions hits they cannot see) and holders on destination chains (they rely on mesh attestations they did not choose). The plan answers both only partly: every denial carries a readable reason through the institution's API, and destination holders can verify supply of record on L1. A complaint and appeal path for frozen positions is now decision D-11: freezes are lifted only by a named legal role through a signed, logged action, and appeals go through the investor's institution under a published SLA, tested in M3.
