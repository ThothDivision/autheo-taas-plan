Build plan (TAAS-DEX)

Development happens in ThothDivision/TAAS-DEX; this page is its plan of record. Milestones M0–M6 sit inside the phases of the Roadmap. Each milestone ends with exit tests a stranger can run; a milestone is not done until they pass in CI and on a shared environment.

Phase 4 (TaaS at scale: self-serve issuance, white-label) is planned after M6 and is not broken into milestones yet.

How work is tested

  • Policy: table-driven test vectors shared between the TypeScript engine and the contract tests, so the off-chain pre-check and the on-chain check cannot drift.
  • Contracts: invariant and fuzz tests; external audit before any mainnet value.
  • Matching: deterministic replay; recorded sessions as regression tests.
  • Settlement: chaos tests (L1 halt, reverted transaction, cash shortfall) must end in Committed or Failed, never in between.
  • Security: STRIDE threat model per service before M2; penetration test before the pilot; post-quantum profile per tenant (Security).

Delivery measures

Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change-failure rate and time to restore, reported per milestone (Accelerate's four key metrics).

Estimate and team (reference class, not a promise)

Reference class: IT projects. Flyvbjerg and Budzier's study of 1,471 IT projects (Why Your IT Project May Be Riskier Than You Think, Harvard Business Review, 2011) found an average cost overrun of 27 %, and one project in six with a cost overrun near 200 % and a schedule overrun near 70 %. This plan uses those figures as its outside view: P50 = inside estimate × 1.3, P80 = × 1.7 (assumption derived from that study, not a forecast for this team). A dedicated reference class of institutional tokenization / DvP builds is not yet assembled (decision D-13).
