Compliance model

Autheo is a technology provider, not a bank, broker-dealer, custodian or exchange. In the target model:

RoleWho
KYC/AML decision, sanctions screeningLicensed providers / the onboarding institution (Autheo enforces the result)
Custody of client assets & keysLicensed custodian partner (MPC/HSM)
Cash leg (tokenized deposits / stablecoin)Partner bank / regulated issuer
Secondary-market venuePartner holding ATS/MTF/RMO licence
Issuer / transfer agent of recordIssuer + licensed transfer agent
Rule enforcement, settlement rails, interoperability, auditAutheo

Rules enforced per movement (src/policy.js)

amount_valid, asset_active (pause/freeze), sender_kyc/receiver_kyc (incl. expiry), sender_sanctions/receiver_sanctions, jurisdiction (allow/deny lists), investor_eligibility (accredited/institution), concentration_limit, lockup, minimum_size, sender_balance. Every result returns all checks with pass/fail and a human-readable detail, which is what an auditor or compliance officer sees.

Sandbox caveats

Seed parties, KYC results, custody and chains are simulated. Nothing here is an offer of securities or a regulated service. Before any live pilot: legal review per jurisdiction, data-protection review (PII stays off-chain; only credential references on-chain), security review and penetration test.
