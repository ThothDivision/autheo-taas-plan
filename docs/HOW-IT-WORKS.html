How it works

This page walks one asset through TaaS end to end — who acts, what state each object is in, what happens when something goes wrong — so a reviewer can check the design without reading code. Terms are defined in the glossary at the bottom; component names match Architecture and the build plan in TAAS-DEX.

The answer in one paragraph

An institution is onboarded once by a licensed KYC provider and receives a credential. An issuer defines an asset together with its rule set (who may hold it, where, how much, when). From then on every movement of that asset — mint, primary sale, secondary fill, coupon, redemption, cross-chain hop — calls the same policy engine, which answers allow or deny with a reason for every check. Trades settle by delivery versus payment (DvP) on Autheo L1: the asset leg and the cash leg (tokenized deposits from a partner bank) both pass the policy check and then move in one transaction, or neither moves. Every decision and movement is written to a hash-chained audit log whose root is anchored on L1. Regulated roles (custody, venue, transfer agent, cash) stay with licensed partners; Autheo runs the rails.

What is on-chain and what is not

On L1: token balances, rule sets, credential references and status (never personal data), DvP settlement records, audit roots, mesh lock/release records. Off-chain: identity documents, KYC files, order book, full audit entries, documents (only their hashes on-chain). This follows privacy by design and keeps erasure requests possible without rewriting the chain.

Glossary

Credential — signed claim from a KYC provider about a party, with expiry. Rule set — the asset's transfer rules. Policy engine — evaluates a proposed movement against the rule set and credentials; returns every check with pass/fail. DvP — delivery versus payment: asset and cash move together or not at all. Tokenized deposit — a bank liability represented on-chain; the institutional cash leg. L0 Mesh — Autheo's cross-chain attestation and routing layer. Supply of record — the canonical balance on Autheo L1. THEO — Autheo's network token (fees, staking), not a cash leg.
